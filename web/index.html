Visible monitoring. Specific choices. Stop at any time.

No content surveillance

No keystrokes, passwords, browser history, screenshots or desktop recordings are collected. Audio is never recorded or saved. The optional pre-flight preview opens the microphone locally to display its level; it sends no audio to the worker.

Optional camera analysis

Frames are processed on the device and discarded. Retained camera metadata includes eye-position consistency, quality and calibration statistics, lighting, face count and multiple-face observations. No face identity is inferred. Camera observations do not change the index.

Local storage for 24 hours

Expired sessions are removed while the worker is running or at its next launch. Exported files remain until you delete them. OS disk encryption is recommended.

Human review, with an alternative

The interviewer should offer an alternative if monitoring is declined and agree any accessibility accommodations. This tool cannot certify fairness or legal compliance.
